The MIT license applies to the application's original source code. Game names,
card/skill data, and game-derived matching artwork and glyph samples retain
their respective owners' rights; they are not relicensed under MIT.

`resources/awakening/`, `modern/resources/live-badges/`, and the `.npz` matching
banks contain game-derived recognition references, not application source.
Shared card/skill catalogs and recognition icons are fetched separately.

Dependencies retain their own licenses. `modern/package-lock.json` records the
frontend dependencies; Python dependencies are listed in `modern/requirements*.txt`.
The release builder collects installed dependency license notices in `LICENSES/`.
This is an unofficial community project, not an official game client.
